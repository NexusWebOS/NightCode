Open [the gallery](index.html) locally to review the four images together. The images contain fictional sample information and permanent SPECIMEN / NOT VALID FOR IDENTIFICATION markings. Back designs use decorative circuit illustrations with no machine-readable data.

The four PNGs in this folder are the original flattened concepts. Editable versions are now available inside Netcon's Badge Studio under **New York specimen · 16-bit** and **New York specimen · polished**. Their generated plates are saved one directory above as `ny-specimen-*-gpt.png`. The supplied official sample cards informed the general ivory/blue palette and front/back format; the generated compositions are original.

Badge Studio accepts details and a portrait, previews either side, saves one shared card record, and exports individual PNG/PDF cards or a two-page PDF. Specimen labels are retained in the generated artwork, including over an inserted portrait. The editor plate prompts are in [editor-prompts.json](editor-prompts.json).

Generated with the built-in GPT image tool. Exact prompts are saved in [prompts.json](prompts.json).
