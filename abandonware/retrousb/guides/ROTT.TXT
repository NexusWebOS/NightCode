RISE OF THE TRIAD - THE HUNT BEGINS (shareware episode) GUIDE
============================================================

A HUNT team is stranded on San Nicolas Island, facing the cult of El
Oscuro. ROTT is fast, loud and full of traps: jump pads, moving
platforms, fire jets, spinning blades and exploding walls.

Controls (change them in SETUP)
-------------------------------
  Arrows move/turn, Ctrl fire, Space open/use, Shift run, Alt strafe,
  1-4 weapons, Tab map, Esc menu. Look up / down and aim are in SETUP.

Pick your hero (from the game's own character table)
----------------------------------------------------
                       health  speed
  Taradino Cassatt      100    medium    the all-rounder
  Thi Barrett            85    fast
  Doug Wendt            150    slowest   tank; a good first pick
  Lorelei Ni             70    fastest   fragile
  Ian Paul Freeley      120    medium-   tough and still quick enough

Weapons
-------
  Pistol, then dual pistols, then the MP40: unlimited-ish bullets.
  Missile weapons (one at a time, limited shots, then gone):
    Bazooka       straight rocket
    Heat-seeker   homes in; best all-purpose missile
    Drunk missile a spread of wobbly rockets; clears rooms
    Firebomb      explodes in a cross of fire
    Flamewall     a wall of fire along the floor
  (Split missile, Dark Staff and the Excalibat are in the full game.)
  Grabbing a new missile weapon replaces the one you carry.

Power-ups
---------
  God mode       invulnerable, you throw energy balls. Enjoy it.
  Mercury mode   fly (look up/down to steer)
  Shrooms mode   the screen goes psychedelic; you're fine
  Elasto mode    you bounce off walls
  Bulletproof armor, Asbestos (fire) armor, Gas mask
  Health: small and big food, monk meals, the first aid kit.

Tactics
-------
  * Walls with cracks or a different look can be blown open.
  * Jump pads (the round pads on the floor) are the way to high ledges.
  * Enemies dodge, roll and play dead. Keep shooting until they gib.
  * Some guards grab your weapon when they are close: don't let them.
  * Save before every trap corridor.
