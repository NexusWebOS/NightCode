THE ELDER SCROLLS: ARENA GUIDE
==============================

The first Elder Scrolls game: all of Tamriel in one game. Bethesda made
it free in 2004.

Getting it onto the stick
-------------------------
  Save Bethesda's free Arena zip (UESP links it) into the builder's
  downloads folder and run the builder again. If the stick shows "first
  run installs", let the installer put it in C:\ARENA.

The story
---------
  The Imperial battlemage Jagar Tharn has imprisoned Emperor Uriel
  Septim VII and taken his place. You start in the Imperial dungeons.
  Ria Silmane, murdered by Tharn, speaks to you in dreams: find the
  eight pieces of the Staff of Chaos, one per province, then face Tharn
  in the Imperial Palace.

  Each staff piece: ask around a city ("where is..." / rumors) to learn
  which dungeon holds the clue, do a small quest for someone, get the
  location of the piece's dungeon, clear it. Ria appears in your sleep
  after each piece and tells you where to look next.

Making your character
---------------------
  * Answer the questions or pick a class. Warrior classes are the
    easiest start: Knight, Warrior, Barbarian.
  * Stats are rolled: re-roll until you get high numbers in the stats
    your class uses (strength and endurance for fighters).
  * Race matters little later; pick what you like.

Surviving the start
-------------------
  * The starting dungeon is hard at level 1. Fight in doorways, rest
    (camp) often, and save a lot, in several slots.
  * Buy better armour as soon as you reach a city.
  * Inns: pay for a room to rest safely in cities. Resting outside at
    night invites monsters.
  * Spells: mages' guilds sell and make spells. A healing spell
    changes the game even for a fighter.
  * Fast travel: the world map. Travel takes game days; that's fine.
  * Talking: ask "Where is" for places; the answers are directions
    ("north of here"). The automap marks what you have seen.

Controls
--------
  Arrow keys move, and the mouse works everything on screen: click the
  icons at the bottom for the map, spells, logbook and status. You
  attack with the right mouse button held while you move the mouse
  across the screen, like swinging the weapon. The keyboard shortcuts
  are in the game's README.
