HERETIC - CITY OF THE DAMNED (shareware episode) GUIDE
=====================================================

You are Corvus, the last Sidhe elf standing against D'Sparil and his
Serpent Riders. Heretic plays like DOOM with magic, an inventory and
the ability to look up and down.

Controls (defaults; SETUP changes them)
---------------------------------------
  Arrows      move / turn          Ctrl    fire
  Alt         strafe               Shift   run
  Space       open / use           1-8     weapons
  PgDn / Del  look up / down       End     look straight ahead again
  [ and ]     pick an inventory item    Enter   use it
  PgUp / Ins  fly up / down (with the Wings of Wrath); Home levels off
  Tab         map                  F2/F3   save / load

The levels
----------
  E1M1 The Docks        E1M4 The Guard Tower   E1M7 The Crypts
  E1M2 The Dungeons     E1M5 The Citadel       E1M8 Hell's Maw
  E1M3 The Gatehouse    E1M6 The Cathedral     E1M9 The Graveyard (secret)

Weapons
-------
  Staff              melee, free
  Gauntlets          melee, drain life when powered up
  Elven Wand         infinite-ish crystal ammo, accurate
  Ethereal Crossbow  three bolts in a spread. The workhorse
  Dragon Claw        rapid ripping shots
  Hellstaff, Phoenix Rod, Firemace come in the later episodes.
  Every weapon changes when the Tome of Power is active (see below).

Artifacts (your inventory)
--------------------------
  Quartz Flask        +25 health          Mystic Urn   full health
  Tome of Power       ~40 s of powered-up weapons: Crossbow and Wand
                      become deadly
  Shadowsphere        near-invisibility: enemies mostly miss
  Ring of Invincibility
  Morph Ovum          turns monsters in front of you into chickens
  Time Bomb of the Ancients   drop it and step away
  Chaos Device        teleports you back to the start of the level
  Torch               light in dark areas
  Wings of Wrath      flight for a while (Home/End)
  Map Scroll          whole level on the map

  Artifacts are kept between levels. Don't hoard Quartz Flasks all the
  way to the end: use them to keep the Tome pushes going.

Enemies
-------
  Gargoyles        flying imps; the red ones shoot fire
  Golems           slow mummies; the ghost kind (Nitrogolems) shoot
  Undead Warriors  throw axes. The red axes hurt a lot
  Disciples of D'Sparil   flying wizards, triple shots. Kill fast
  Weredragons, Ophidians  later heavies
  Iron Liches      the E1M8 bosses: ice storms and whirlwinds. Tome +
                   Crossbow, keep moving, use cover pillars.
  Ghost enemies (see-through) ignore some attacks; crossbow works.

Tactics
-------
  * Tome of Power + Crossbow before any big fight is the Heretic combo.
  * Morph Ovum against Undead Warriors and Disciples saves health.
  * Look up and down: Gargoyles attack from above.
  * Keys are yellow, green and blue. The key colour shows on door frames.

Cheats (type during play)
-------------------------
  QUICKEN     god mode        RAMBO     all weapons and ammo
  SKEL        all keys        KITTY     walk through walls
  PONCE       full health     MASSACRE  kill every monster
  RAVMAP      (on the map) reveal it
  ENGAGEem    warp to episode e map m, e.g. ENGAGE15
  GIMMExn     artifact x (a-j) times n (1-9)
  COCKADOODLEDOO   become a chicken (and back)
  IDDQD / IDKFA    try them. Raven's joke on DOOM players.
