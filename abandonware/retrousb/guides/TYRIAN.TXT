TYRIAN 2000 GUIDE
=================

A vertical shoot-'em-up with a real story, a shop between levels and
five episodes: Escape, Treachery, Mission: Suicide, An End to Fate and
Hazudra Fodder (the one added in Tyrian 2000).

Controls
--------
  Arrows or mouse move, Space fire, Enter changes the rear weapon's
  mode, Alt and Ctrl fire the left and right sidekicks. Esc pauses.
  Setup lets you change all of it.

Game modes
----------
  Full game    the story, with the shop and datacubes. Start here.
  Arcade       power-ups drop during the level, no shop.
  2 Player Arcade, and more modes that unlock later.

The shop (Full game)
--------------------
  Front gun     your main weapon; upgrade its power level
  Rear gun      covers the sides and back; several fire modes
  Sidekicks     left and right helpers; some charge up, some fire
                on their own
  Generator     THE most important part. Every shot uses energy, and
                the generator refills it. A weak generator means your
                guns stop firing in long fights.
  Shield        regenerates, powered by the generator too
  Ship          better hulls carry more armor

  Order that works: generator first, then the front gun's power, then
  the shield. Sidekicks last.

Tips
----
  * Shoot everything, including buildings and scenery. Hidden credits
    and power-ups are everywhere.
  * Datacubes tell the story. Read them in the shop menu.
  * Some levels have secret branches: destroy special targets or fly
    through odd places to open new routes on the map.
  * Your armor does not refill between levels by itself; the shield
    does during a level. Buy armor repairs when low.
  * Bosses have patterns: learn them on Easy first.

Also free: OpenTyrian is a modern open-source version that plays these
same data files on Windows, Linux and phones.
