COMMAND & CONQUER: RED ALERT GUIDE
=================================

Einstein removes Hitler from history and the Soviet Union marches on
Europe. Allies against Soviets. EA made it free in 2008.

Getting it onto the stick
-------------------------
  Two disc images: Allied and Soviet. Save them (ISO, or a zip holding
  the ISO) into the builder's downloads folder and run the builder again.
  The first run mounts the disc as E: and starts its installer: install
  into C:\REDALERT.

The basics
----------
  * Money: ore trucks mine ore (and gems, worth more) and bring it to
    the refinery. More trucks and a second refinery early.
  * Power: build power plants ahead of need; low power disables
    defences and slows building.
  * Build order: Power plant, Barracks, Ore refinery, Power plant,
    War factory, second Refinery, Service depot.
  * Naval yards exist on water maps: submarines, destroyers, gunboats.

Allies
------
  Light and medium tanks, artillery, Rangers (jeeps), Tanya (commando,
  destroys buildings with C4, shoots infantry in one shot), Spies (steal
  money, see inside buildings), Engineers, Medics, Mechanics,
  Gap Generator (hides your base), GPS satellite (reveals the map),
  the Chronosphere (teleport units) and cruisers at sea.
  * Medium tanks in numbers with a few rocket soldiers.
  * Tanya plus a Chinook or APC ends many missions early.
  * Cruisers wreck coastal bases from far out.

Soviets
-------
  Heavy tanks, Mammoth tanks, V2 rockets, MiGs and Yaks, attack dogs
  (kill infantry and sniff out spies), flamethrowers, Tesla coils and
  Tesla tanks, the Iron Curtain (invulnerability) and nukes.
  * Heavy tanks are cost-effective: mass them.
  * Tesla coils are the best base defence in the game, if you have
    the power for them.
  * A few attack dogs in your base stop spies and Tanya.

Tricks
------
  * Ctrl+number stores a team, the number recalls it. Alt forces a
    move through anything; Ctrl forces fire on the ground or your own
    units.
  * Barrels explode: shoot them near enemies.
  * Service depot repairs vehicles; a Mechanic (Allies) repairs in the
    field.
  * Save at the start of each mission.
