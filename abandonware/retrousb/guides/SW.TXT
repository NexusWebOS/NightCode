SHADOW WARRIOR (shareware) GUIDE
===============================

Lo Wang, bodyguard turned ninja, against the Zilla corporation's
demons. Build engine like Duke 3D, with swords, ninjas and very bad
jokes.

First: run SETUP (S in the DOS menu) for sound and controls.

Controls (original defaults)
----------------------------
  Arrows move/turn, Ctrl fire, Space open, Shift run, CapsLock auto-run,
  A jump, Z crouch, Alt strafe, , and . strafe left/right,
  PgUp/PgDn look, Home/End aim, 1-0 weapons, ' and ; next/previous.
  [ ] Enter  inventory
  M medkit  S smoke bomb  N night vision  G gas bomb  F flash bomb
  C caltrops  T message line (for cheats)  Tab map

  Pressing a weapon's number again switches its alternate mode (for
  example the rocket launcher's heat-seeker and nuke).

The levels
----------
  1 Seppuku Station             3 Master Leep's Temple
  2 Zilla Construction          4 Dark Woods of the Serpent

Weapons
-------
  Fists and Katana (sword), Shurikens, Riot gun (shotgun), Uzi (two
  if you find a second), Missile launcher, Grenade launcher, Sticky
  bombs, Rail gun, Guardian head, Ripper heart.
  The shareware has the first several; the rest are in the full game.

  * The katana cuts most enemies in one or two swings.
  * Riot gun with the TurboDrive upgrade fires four shells at once.
  * Sticky bombs stick to walls and enemies: set traps at corners.

Inventory
---------
  Portable medkit   heal 100 on demand
  Smoke bomb        become nearly invisible for a while
  Night vision, Gas bomb, Flash bomb (blinds enemies), Caltrops
  (spikes on the floor behind you)
  Fortune cookie: +50 health (over the maximum)
  Armor vest +50, Kevlar +100, Medkit +20

Enemies
-------
  Ninjas       brown ones are weak, red ones are fast; some crawl
  Coolies      walk at you and explode; shoot them from far away
  Coolie ghosts, Hornets (swarms; sword them), Guardians (fireballs),
  Accursed, Ripper hearts, Serpent god... the later ones are nasty.

Cheats (press T, type the code, press Enter)
--------------------------------------------
  SWCHAN    god mode             SWGIMME  all weapons and items
  SWGHOST   walk through walls   SWTREKnn warp to level nn
  SWGREED   god + everything     SWSTART  restart the level
  SWLOC     position and frame rate    SWMAP  full map
  (From the game's own cheat table.)

After the shareware
-------------------
