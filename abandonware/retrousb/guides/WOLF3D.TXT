WOLFENSTEIN 3D - ESCAPE FROM WOLFENSTEIN (shareware episode) GUIDE
=================================================================

You are B.J. Blazkowicz, locked in Castle Wolfenstein. Nine floors
stand between you and the way out, plus one secret floor.

Controls (defaults; change under Customize in the menu)
-------------------------------------------------------
  Arrows    move / turn          Ctrl    fire
  Alt       strafe (hold)        Shift   run (hold)
  Space     open doors, push walls, press elevator switches
  1-4       knife, pistol, machine gun, chain gun
  Esc       menu (save, load, sound, controls)

Floors
------
  Floors 1-8, then Hans Grosse on floor 9.
  Floor 10 is the secret floor: its elevator is hidden behind a push
  wall on floor 1. Push the walls as you go.

Weapons
-------
  Knife        silent, only good for dogs and sleeping guards
  Pistol       starting gun, ok for single guards
  Machine gun  dropped by SS guards. Your main weapon
  Chain gun    found in secret areas; eats ammo, shreds bosses
  All guns share one ammo pool (max 99). Guards drop clips.

Enemies
-------
  Guards (brown)   weak, slow to react. Pistol is enough.
  Dogs             fast, bite. Knife or pistol at close range.
  SS (blue)        tough, drop the machine gun. Hit them first.
  Hans Grosse      floor 9 boss, dual chain guns, lots of health.
                   Use the chain gun, fight from a doorway, step out,
                   burst, step back.

Health and points
-----------------
  Dog food +4    Plate of food +10    First aid kit +25
  Blood puddles +1 (only when you are below 11 health)
  One Up: full health, 25 ammo and an extra life
  Treasure: cross 100, chalice 500, chest 1000, crown 5000 points
  An extra life every 40,000 points.
  The end of each floor scores kills, secrets and treasure %.

Secrets: push walls
-------------------
  Walk up to walls and press Space. A wall that slides away is a push
  wall. Good places to try:
    * walls with a picture or a flag in the middle of a long corridor
    * any wall that looks different from its neighbours
    * dead ends that make no sense
  Push walls hide treasure, food, the chain gun and the floor 10
  elevator on floor 1.

Tactics
-------
  * Gunfire alerts every guard in connected rooms. Expect a rush after
    the first shot; back into a corridor and let them come one by one.
  * Open a door, step aside, let the guards run into the doorway.
  * Guards shoot less accurately at a distance and when you move.
  * Don't waste the chain gun on guards; keep ammo for the SS and Hans.

Cheat (hold the keys down together)
-----------------------------------
  M + L + I   full health, 99 ammo, both keys, all weapons.
              Your score is reset to 0 for it.
