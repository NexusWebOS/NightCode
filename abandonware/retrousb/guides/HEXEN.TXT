HEXEN: BEYOND HERETIC (demo) GUIDE
==================================

Hexen is Heretic grown up: you pick a class, the world is made of hubs
(groups of maps you travel between), and the way forward is often a
switch or a puzzle item in another map. The demo has the opening maps of
the first hub.

Controls
--------
  As in Heretic: arrows, Ctrl fire, Space use, PgDn/Del look up/down,
  End centre view, [ ] Enter inventory, PgUp/Ins fly, Tab map.
  / (slash) jumps.

The three classes
-----------------
  Fighter (Baratus)   most health and armor, strongest in melee.
                      Spiked Gauntlets, Timon's Axe, Hammer of
                      Retribution, Quietus.
  Cleric (Parias)     middle of the road; good at range and close.
                      Mace of Contrition, Serpent Staff (drains life at
                      close range), Firestorm, Wraithverge.
  Mage (Daedolon)     fragile, but kills from far away.
                      Sapphire Wand (free), Frost Shards, Arcs of Death,
                      Bloodscourge.
  The fourth weapon of each class is built from three pieces spread
  over the full game. Weapons 2 and 3 use blue and green mana.

  First time: play the Fighter. He forgives mistakes.

How the hubs work
-----------------
  * You go back and forth between the maps of a hub through portals.
    Monsters you killed stay dead; switches you pressed stay pressed.
  * Messages like "One third of the puzzle has been solved" mean a
    switch changed something somewhere, maybe in another map. Go back
    and look for what opened.
  * Puzzle items (gems, skulls, books, gears) go into matching
    sockets. They show at the bottom of the inventory.
  * Read the automap (Tab); new openings show there.
  * Save often, in different slots.

Items
-----
  Quartz Flask +25     Mystic Urn: full health
  Crystal Vial small health     Mystic Ambit Incant: helps everyone
  Icon of the Defender  invulnerability (each class gets its own twist)
  Porkalator            turns monsters into pigs
  Banishment Device     teleports a monster away
  Dark Servant          summons a minotaur on your side
  Disc of Repulsion     pushes enemies and missiles away
  Flechette             a throwable potion; different for each class
  Boots of Speed, Krater of Might (mana), Dragonskin Bracers (armor),
  Torch, Wings of Wrath, Chaos Device (back to the hub start)

  Armor comes in four pieces (amulet, helmet, bracers, mesh armor) and
  counts differently for each class.

Enemies
-------
  Ettins     big two-headed maces; simple, but hit hard
  Afrits     fire gargoyles, fast and flying
  Centaurs   block with shields: attack when the shield is down
  Serpents   in the water and slime; strike when they surface
  Wendigos   ice creatures that freeze you
  Dark Bishops, Reivers, Stalkers: later on
