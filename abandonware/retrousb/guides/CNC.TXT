COMMAND & CONQUER GUIDE
======================

GDI against the Brotherhood of Nod, fighting over tiberium. EA made the
game free in 2007.

Getting it onto the stick
-------------------------
  The free release is two disc images: GDI and Nod. Save them (ISO, or a
  zip holding the ISO) into the builder's downloads folder and run the
  builder again. The first run mounts the disc as E: and starts its
  installer: install into C:\CNC. Keep the disc image on the stick; the
  game wants it for movies and music.

The basics
----------
  * Money comes from tiberium: harvesters collect it and bring it to
    the refinery. A refinery comes with one harvester. Build a second
    refinery or harvester early.
  * Power: every building needs it. When power runs low, production
    slows and defences stop. Build power plants ahead of need.
  * Build order that works: Power plant, Barracks / Hand of Nod,
    Refinery, second Power plant, War Factory / Airstrip, second
    Refinery.
  * Protect your harvesters. Losing them ends your income.
  * Tiberium hurts infantry who walk through it.

Controls
--------
  Left-click selects, left-click again moves or attacks. Drag a box to
  select many. Ctrl+number stores a team, the number recalls it.
  Right-click cancels. The sidebar builds things: click an icon, then
  place the building next to your base.

GDI
---
  Strengths: armour. Medium tank, then the Mammoth tank (slow, very
  tough, heals itself a little). Orca helicopters, A-10 airstrikes, and
  the Ion Cannon from the Advanced Communications Center.
  * Mass medium tanks with a few rocket soldiers. Grenadiers for
    infantry.
  * Guard towers and advanced guard towers hold chokepoints.

Nod
---
  Strengths: speed and tricks. Recon bikes, buggies, flame tanks,
  stealth tanks, the Obelisk of Light and the nuke from the Temple of
  Nod.
  * Stealth tanks hunt harvesters and lone buildings.
  * Flame tanks and flamethrower infantry melt GDI infantry.
  * An Obelisk covering your base entrance stops most early attacks.

Tricks
------
  * Engineers capture enemy buildings. Capture a refinery or the
    Construction Yard to change a mission.
  * Sandbags and walls funnel the enemy AI into your defences.
  * Selling a building returns part of its cost in an emergency.
  * The AI attacks along the same paths: defend those.
  * Save at the start of each mission and mid-way.
