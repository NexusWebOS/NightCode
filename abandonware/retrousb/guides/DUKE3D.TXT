DUKE NUKEM 3D - L.A. MELTDOWN (shareware episode) GUIDE
======================================================

Aliens have invaded Los Angeles. Duke is not amused.

First: run SETUP (S in the DOS menu) and choose Sound Blaster for
effects and music, then your controls.

Controls (original defaults)
----------------------------
  Arrows      move / turn        Ctrl       fire
  Space       open / use         Shift      run    CapsLock  auto-run
  A           jump               Z          crouch
  Alt         strafe             , and .    strafe left / right
  PgUp/PgDn   look up / down     Home/End   aim up / down
  1-0         weapons            ' and ;    next / previous weapon
  [ ] Enter   pick / use inventory item
  H holoduke  J jetpack  N night vision  M medkit  R steroids
  `           quick kick         Backspace  turn around
  Tab         map                F2/F3      save / load

The levels
----------
  E1L1 Hollywood Holocaust    E1L4 Toxic Dump
  E1L2 Red Light District     E1L5 The Abyss (boss: the Battlelord)
  E1L3 Death Row              E1L6 Launch Facility (secret)

Weapons
-------
  Mighty Foot   always available; quick kick (`) works with any gun
  Pistol        accurate, fast reload
  Shotgun       best close-range gun
  Chaingun      burns ammo, shreds troopers
  RPG           huge damage; never at close range
  Pipe bombs    throw, then press fire again to detonate
  Shrinker      shrink an enemy, then step on it
  Devastator, laser tripbombs and the Freezethrower: later episodes
  (freeze an enemy, then kick it to pieces)

Items
-----
  Portable medkit (M)  heals on demand; saves you in boss fights
  Steroids (R)   fast and strong for a while; also cures being shrunk
  Holoduke (H)   a decoy Duke; enemies shoot it
  Jetpack (J)    fly; Jump/Crouch go up/down
  Night vision (N), Scuba gear (automatic underwater),
  Protective boots (walk on toxic slime), Armor, Atomic health (+50,
  up to 200)

Enemies
-------
  Assault troopers   the pig-lizard grunts; some teleport
  Pig cops           shotguns; hit hard at close range
  Octabrains         float; their mind blast hurts. Kill at range
  Enforcers / Assault captains (later), Sentry drones (explode)
  Battlelord         E1L5 boss: chaingun and mortars. Stay far away,
                     circle, use RPG and pipe bombs, medkit when low.

Secrets and tactics
-------------------
  * Cracked walls blow open with pipe bombs or RPG rockets.
  * Press Space on everything: light switches, toilets, posters, walls.
  * Vents: kick or shoot the grate.
  * Explosive canisters kill groups; keep your distance.
  * Pipe bomb a doorway, wait for the enemy, detonate.
  * Shrunk enemies grow back after a while: squash fast.

Cheats (type during play; all start with DN)
--------------------------------------------
  DNKROZ / DNCORNHOLIO   god mode      DNSTUFF    everything
  DNWEAPONS  weapons     DNITEMS   items     DNKEYS   keys
  DNINVENTORY inventory  DNCLIP    walk through walls
  DNSCOTTYel  warp to episode e, level l  (DNSCOTTY15 = The Abyss)
  DNVIEW     third-person view            DNHYPER   steroids
  DNSHOWMAP  full map    DNUNLOCK  open all doors
  DNMONSTERS monsters on/off              DNSKILLn  restart on skill n
  (From the game's own cheat table.)
