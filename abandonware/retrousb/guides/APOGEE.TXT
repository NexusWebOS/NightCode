APOGEE CLASSICS GUIDE
=====================
Duke Nukem, Duke Nukem II, Commander Keen 1 and 4, Cosmo, Bio Menace,
Blake Stone and Raptor.

Apogee's shareware model: the first episode free, the rest by mail.
Every game here is that free first episode, except Bio Menace, whose
three episodes were made freeware in 2005.

Most of them start with a short menu: press Enter or Space to begin,
F1 for help (the in-game help lists every key), Esc for the menu.

Duke Nukem (1991) - Shrapnel City
---------------------------------
  Arrows move, Ctrl jump, Alt fire, Up arrow uses doors and switches.
  * Dr. Proton's robots are everywhere. Shoot them before they reach you.
  * Collect D, U, K, E in that order for a big bonus.
  * The boots let you jump higher, the claws let you hang from
    ceilings, the robohand works some machines. Keep your eye out
    for them: some levels can't be finished without them.
  * Keys open the matching locked doors. Hint globes explain new things.
  * Food and soda restore health. Shooting soda cans launches them.

Duke Nukem II (1993)
--------------------
  Arrows move, Ctrl jump, Alt fire, Up arrow uses things.
  * The Rigelatins kidnapped Duke. Find the level exit, sometimes after
    destroying all the radar dishes (a message tells you).
  * Weapon pickups (laser, rocket launcher, flame thrower) replace the
    normal gun until their ammo runs out.
  * Crouch (Down) under enemy fire. Many enemies can't shoot low.

Commander Keen 1 (1990) - Marooned on Mars
------------------------------------------
  Arrows move, Ctrl jump, Alt pogo stick (after you find it),
  Ctrl + Alt together fires the raygun.
  * Billy Blaze's ship is missing four parts: a joystick, a battery,
    a vacuum cleaner and a bottle of Everclear. Each is in a level on the
    Mars map. Get all four and fly home.
  * The pogo stick is found in one of the first levels: go and get it,
    it makes every other level possible.
  * Yorps (one-eyed) just push you. Gargs charge: shoot them or jump.
    Vorticons take several shots.
  * Collect raygun charges; you start with none.
  * Cheat: hold C, T and Space together for keycards, pogo and ammo.

Commander Keen 4 (1991) - Secret of the Oracle
----------------------------------------------
  Arrows move, Ctrl jump, Alt pogo, Space fires the neural stunner.
  Up enters doors and switches. Down on the world map enters levels.
  * Rescue the eight Council members hidden across the Shadowlands.
  * Drops of lifewater: 100 give an extra life. Points too, every 20,000.
  * Enemies are stunned, not killed: stunned Slugs and Mad Mushrooms don't
    stay down forever. Most things can be jumped over.
  * The Dopefish lives in the Well of Wishes. Don't let it eat you.
  * Save at the world map before every level (F3 menu or Esc -> Save).
  * Cheat: hold B, A and T together for all items, full ammo and keys.

Cosmo's Cosmic Adventure (1992) - Forbidden Planet
--------------------------------------------------
  Arrows move, Ctrl jump, Alt drops a bomb, Up uses things.
  * Cosmo climbs walls with his suction-cup hands: jump against a wall
    and hold toward it.
  * Jump on most enemies to kill them, or bomb them.
  * Hint globes (shoot or touch them) explain every new trick.
  * Stars are bonus points; hidden stars are everywhere.

Bio Menace (1993, freeware) - three episodes
--------------------------------------------
  Arrows move, Ctrl jump, Alt fire, Space throws a grenade, Up uses.
  * Snake Logan against Dr. Mangle's mutants in Metro City.
  * Rescue the hostages: they often hold keycards or give hints.
  * Super bullets and the machine gun are pickups; grenades blow up
    walls and groups.
  * Crystal shards unlock the level exits in some levels.

Blake Stone: Aliens of Gold (1993)
----------------------------------
  Wolf3D engine: arrows, Ctrl fire, Space use, Alt strafe, Shift run.
  * Blake Stone against Dr. Pyrus Goldfire's alien mutants.
  * Scientists in lab coats are informants: walk up and press Space to
    talk, don't shoot them. Some are armed traitors; they shoot first.
  * Collect access cards: red, yellow, blue open the matching floors
    and doors. The elevator only goes to floors you have the card for.
  * The automap is one of the best of the era: use it.
  * Food tokens buy snacks at vending machines.

Raptor: Call of the Shadows (1994) - Bravo Sector
-------------------------------------------------
  Mouse or keyboard; the mouse is best. Fire with the left button,
  change special weapons with the right button (Space on keys).
  * Between missions you're in the hangar: buy weapons and shields,
    sell what you don't use.
  * Buy Mega bombs early; they clear the screen in an emergency.
  * Destroy ground targets for money, but don't chase them into fire.
  * Your shield (the bar) refills only by buying or picking up shield
    power-ups. Energy shields stack.
