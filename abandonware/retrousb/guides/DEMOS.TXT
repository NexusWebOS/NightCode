FREE SPECIALS AND DEMOS GUIDE
=============================
Jazz Jackrabbit: Holiday Hare '95, Theme Hospital (demo), Heroes of
Might and Magic II (demo), Spear of Destiny (demo: see WOLF3D.TXT).

Jazz Jackrabbit: Holiday Hare '95
---------------------------------
  Epic gave this holiday special away for free.
  Arrows move, Ctrl jump, Alt fire (check SETUP for your version).
  * Jazz is fast: running makes jumps longer.
  * Carrots restore health. Gems are points.
  * Blaster ammo types: collect the colored ammo to switch; the normal
    blaster never runs out.
  * Find the level's signpost to finish it. Speed-run and explore:
    hidden areas are full of gems.

Theme Hospital (demo)
---------------------
  Bullfrog's funniest sim. Build a hospital, cure patients, make money.
  Mouse only. The demo is one level.

  Order that works
  * Reception desk and a receptionist first; no receptionist, no
    patients.
  * GP's office, then a pharmacy and a nurse, then a general diagnosis
    room and a ward.
  * The Inflation room cures Bloaty Head; the Slack Tongue clinic cures
    Slack Tongue.
  * Benches, drinks machines, radiators and plants keep people happy.
    Toilets matter.
  * Hire a handyman early: vomit and litter drive patients away.
  * Build staff rooms or doctors get tired and quit.
  * Watch the queue at the GP's; a second GP's office is cheap and
    doubles your throughput.

Heroes of Might and Magic II (demo)
-----------------------------------
  One scenario (Broken Alliance) of New World Computing's best Heroes.
  Mouse only.

  * Every week your castle's dwellings produce new creatures: build
    the dwellings early, recruit every week.
  * Capture mines (gold, wood, ore, and the rare resources) as soon as
    you can. Gold is king.
  * Keep one strong main hero with the army and a cheap second hero to
    flag mines and ferry creatures.
  * A Mage Guild lets heroes learn spells. Knowledge and Spell Power
    raise how often and how hard they cast.
  * In battle: shooters behind, strongest melee in front, hit enemy
    shooters first. Wait (W) lets a slow creature act last.
  * Don't leave your castle empty; enemy heroes walk right in.
