THE BEST OF THE REST - DOS CLASSICS WORTH OWNING
==============================================

Most "abandonware" is not abandoned: somebody still owns it, and many of
the best DOS games are sold today for a few dollars, usually already set
up for DOSBox. Buy them on GOG (DRM-free, the files are yours to keep)
or Steam, then put each game's folder in retrousb\mygames and run the
builder again: they go onto the stick next to the free ones.

What's sold changes, so check the store. At the time of writing these
were available on GOG and/or Steam:
