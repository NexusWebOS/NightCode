DOOM - KNEE-DEEP IN THE DEAD (shareware episode) STRATEGY GUIDE
==============================================================

Controls (original defaults; change them in SETUP)
--------------------------------------------------
  Arrows    move / turn           Ctrl     fire
  Alt       strafe (hold)         Shift    run (hold)
  Space     open doors, press switches
  1-7       weapons               Tab      automap
  F2 / F3   save / load           F6 / F9  quick save / quick load
  - / +     smaller / bigger view F5       detail level
  Esc       menu

  Tip: hold Shift most of the time. Speed wins fights in DOOM.

The levels
----------
  E1M1 Hangar             E1M4 Command Control     E1M7 Computer Station
  E1M2 Nuclear Plant      E1M5 Phobos Lab          E1M8 Phobos Anomaly
  E1M3 Toxin Refinery     E1M6 Central Processing  E1M9 Military Base
  E1M9 is the secret level: its exit is hidden in E1M3.

Weapons (shareware has 1-5 plus the chainsaw)
---------------------------------------------
  1 Fist / Chainsaw   Chainsaw is great against demons: they can't bite
                      back while it stuns them.
  2 Pistol            Accurate on the first shot only. Use the chaingun.
  3 Shotgun           Your main weapon. Point blank it kills imps in one.
  4 Chaingun          Fast, accurate, good at range.
  5 Rocket launcher   Huge damage, blast radius hurts you too. Never fire
                      it at an enemy standing next to you.
  (Plasma rifle and BFG 9000 are in the registered episodes.)

Enemies of episode 1 (health)
-----------------------------
  Zombieman        20   drops a clip
  Shotgun guy      30   drops a shotgun; kill these first, they hit hard
  Imp              60   throws fireballs you can sidestep
  Demon / Spectre 150   melee only; back off and shotgun. Spectres are
                        the see-through ones.
  Baron of Hell  1000   two guard E1M8. Rockets from far away.

Items
-----
  Stimpack +10  Medikit +25  Health bonus +1 (can go past 100)
  Green armor 100 (stops 1/3 of damage)  Blue armor 200 (stops 1/2)
  Soulsphere +100 health (up to 200)     Backpack: double ammo capacity
  Berserk: fist does ten times damage and health goes to 100
  Radiation suit: walk on green slime and lava without damage
  Invulnerability, Partial invisibility, Light goggles, Computer map

Tactics that matter
-------------------
  * Strafe. Moving sideways dodges every projectile in the game.
    Running while strafing is faster than running straight.
  * Monsters fight each other. If an imp's fireball hits a zombie, they
    turn on each other. Stand so they hit each other.
  * Barrels explode. Shoot one near a crowd; chains of barrels clear
    rooms.
  * Doors are cover: fight from a doorway, step back to let it close.
  * Sound wakes monsters in connected rooms. Punching is silent...
    but your gun is not. Expect company after the first shot.
  * Use the automap (Tab). Unexplored doors and odd wall textures show
    secrets. Walls with a different texture, or a slight misalignment,
    often open with Space.
  * Save before every big room (F6 quick save).

E1M8 Phobos Anomaly
-------------------
  Two Barons of Hell wait in the star-shaped arena. Keep far away, use
  rockets or the chaingun, and circle so they don't both hit you. When the
  walls drop and you teleport into the dark room, the episode ends as your
  health runs down: that is the story, not a bug.

Cheats (type during play, no Enter)
-----------------------------------

  IDFA         weapons and ammo   IDSPISPOPD  walk through walls
               (IDCLIP is the DOOM II spelling)
  IDCLEV1x     warp to E1Mx (shareware: episode 1 only)
  IDBEHOLDx    x = V invuln, S berserk, I invisible, R rad suit,
               A map, L light goggles
  IDDT         (on the automap) show the whole map, then the monsters
  IDMYPOS      your coordinates

After the shareware
-------------------
  The full DOOM (Ultimate DOOM) and DOOM II are sold on Steam and GOG; put
  your copy's folder in mygames. Freedoom is a free replacement for the
  data files, playable with the same program.
