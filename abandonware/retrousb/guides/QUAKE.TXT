QUAKE - DIMENSION OF THE DOOMED (shareware episode) GUIDE
========================================================

Slipgates, Lovecraftian monsters and full 3D. The shareware is episode 1.

Make the controls modern (do this first)
----------------------------------------
  Press ~ (the key left of 1) to open the console and type, one line at a
  time, Enter after each:
      bind w +forward
      bind s +back
      bind a +moveleft
      bind d +moveright
      bind space +jump
      bind mouse1 +attack
      +mlook
  Add the same lines to ID1\AUTOEXEC.CFG so they stay. Options in the menu
  also let you turn on "Mouse look" and "Always run".

  Speed in DOSBox: Quake is heavy. Press - to shrink the view, or pick a
  low video mode in Options. 320x200 plays fine.

Choosing the skill
------------------
  The start map is a hub: walk through a hallway to pick Easy, Normal or
  Hard, then the episode 1 gate. Nightmare is hidden in the start map.

The levels
----------
  E1M1 The Slipgate Complex     E1M5 Gloom Keep
  E1M2 Castle of the Damned     E1M6 The Door to Chthon
  E1M3 The Necropolis           E1M7 The House of Chthon (boss)
  E1M4 The Grisly Grotto        E1M8 Ziggurat Vertigo (secret)

Weapons
-------
  1 Axe          2 Shotgun           3 Double-barrelled shotgun
  4 Nailgun      5 Perforator        6 Grenade launcher
  7 Rocket launcher                  8 Thunderbolt
  * Grenades bounce: lob them around corners.
  * Never fire the Thunderbolt under water: it discharges and kills you
    (and everyone near).

Enemies
-------
  Grunts, Rottweilers     easy; shotgun
  Enforcers               lasers you can dodge
  Knights / Death Knights sword charge; Death Knights throw fire
  Ogres                   grenades and a chainsaw; fight close so the
                          grenades miss, or snipe with nails
  Zombies                 only die when gibbed: grenades or rockets.
                          Bullets just knock them down for a while.
  Scrags                  flying, spit; nailgun
  Fiends                  leap across the room; sidestep, then shoot
  Shamblers               600 health, lightning; explosives do half
                          damage to them. Use cover and nails.
  Rotfish                 in water; annoying, weak

Power-ups
---------
  Quad Damage (x4 damage), Pentagram of Protection (invulnerable),
  Ring of Shadows (nearly invisible), Biosuit (breathe underwater and in
  slime). Armor: green 100, yellow 150, red 200. Megahealth +100.

Chthon (E1M7)
-------------
  Your weapons do nothing to him. Around the lava lake are buttons:
  press them to charge the electrodes and hit him with lightning. It
  takes more than one strike. Keep moving; his lava balls hurt.

Console commands (~)
--------------------
  god          god mode              give h 100   health
  impulse 9    all weapons and ammo  impulse 255  Quad Damage
  noclip       walk through walls    notarget     monsters ignore you
  fly          fly                   map e1m8     go to a map
  skill 3      Nightmare (then: map e1m1)

After the shareware
-------------------
  Quake (all four episodes and both mission packs) is sold on Steam and
  GOG.
