FREEDOOM GUIDE
==============

Freedoom is a complete, free game built for the DOOM engine: new maps,
monsters, sounds and music, all under the BSD licence. It comes in two
parts:

  Phase 1   four episodes of 9 levels, in the style of the original DOOM
  Phase 2   32 levels in one long campaign, in the style of DOOM II

On this stick it runs with Crispy Doom, a Windows port of the original
engine with higher resolution and quality-of-life options. No DOSBox.

Starting
--------
  START-HERE.bat, then Freedoom: Phase 1 or Phase 2.
  To change controls, video or sound, run GAMES\FREEDOOM\crispy-doom-
  setup.exe (its settings stay on the stick, next to the game).

Controls (Crispy Doom defaults; change them in the setup program)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
  Arrows / WASD with mouse look if you turn it on in setup
  Ctrl or left mouse   fire          Space   open doors, press switches
  Shift                run           Alt     strafe
  1-7                  weapons       Tab     automap
  F2 / F3              save / load   Esc     menu

  In the menu, Crispness (under Options) has the extras: mouse look,
  jumping, crosshair, higher resolution, widescreen.

How it plays
------------
  Freedoom follows DOOM's rules exactly, so DOOM.TXT's tactics apply:
  * strafe to dodge projectiles, run most of the time;
  * let monsters hit each other;
  * explosive barrels clear groups;
  * the shotgun is your workhorse, rockets are for the big ones, never
    at point-blank range.

  The monsters are new designs, but each matches a DOOM monster's
  strength: if something looks big and slow, treat it like a Baron of
  Hell and use rockets from far away.

Weapons (same slots as DOOM)
----------------------------
  1 fist / chainsaw   2 pistol   3 shotgun (and double shotgun in
  Phase 2)   4 minigun   5 missile launcher   6 plasma weapon
  7 the big one (the BFG slot)

Cheats (type during play; same as DOOM II)
------------------------------------------
  IDDQD god mode      IDKFA everything
  IDCLIP walk through walls (Phase 1: IDSPISPOPD)
  IDCLEVxy / IDCLEVxx warp (Phase 1: episode x map y; Phase 2: map xx)

Also works with
---------------
  Any DOOM source port (GZDoom, Chocolate Doom, PrBoom+, ...) on any
  system: they all take freedoom1.wad and freedoom2.wad as IWADs.
